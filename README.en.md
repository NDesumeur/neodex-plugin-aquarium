# Aquarium

A relaxed management game that extends **Pêche** (Fishing): the fish you catch arrive alive in your aquarium. Put them in suitable tanks, feed them, keep the water clean, decorate, and visitors come to admire them, leaving coins that you can also spend in Fishing.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## How it works

1. **The holding pen**: every fish caught in Fishing arrives there alive (60 at most). On your first visit it receives one fish of every species already in your collection.
2. **Tanks**: pick a fish in the pen and click **Put** to place it in the tank on screen. It needs the right water: fresh (pond, river, lake), sea, tropical, polar or abyss, depending on where the species lives. Fish over 60 cm need a medium tank, those over 120 cm a large one.
3. **Care**: fish get hungry and the water gets dirty, **even while NeoDex is closed**. Three gauges at the top of the tank show it: **Hunger**, **Dirty** and **Joy**. **Feed** drops food that the fish swim to, **Clean** clears the water (otherwise it turns green).
4. **Visitors**: happy fish draw a crowd. You see them walk past the glass, stop in front of the finest fish and love them (a heart). Sometimes they drop a **tip** on the floor: click it before it vanishes. Their coins pile up in the jar; click the jar or **Collect** to move them into your Fishing purse. When you come back, the game tells you what visitors left while you were away.
5. **Living fish**: well-fed fish **grow** (about 20 days from smallest to biggest, as far as the tank allows). Two adults of the same species in a happy tank have **young**, sometimes of a **rare colour** worth three times more. Big fish are drawn bigger.

## Laying out a tank

- **Shape**: rectangle (starting shape), rounded (800 coins, joy +5, children love it), column (1,200 coins, visitors +15 %, 2 fewer decorations) or panoramic (2,000 coins, visitors +25 %, 2 more decorations).
- **Living plants**: water grass, water fern and tall kelp, six at most per tank. They **grow** when the water is clean and clean it in return. Drag them along the sand. A plant can be uprooted.

## The ecosystem

Each species has its own way of life, written next to its name:

- **predators** (pike, catfish, shark…) may eat fish much smaller than themselves, above all when hungry: keep them apart or feed them;
- **cleaners** (crayfish, bottom fish…) keep the water clean for longer;
- some **eat plants**, which then struggle to grow.

The **Life of the tank** box sums up what is going on.

## Visitors and their tastes

Four kinds of visitors come by: the **child** likes colourful fish, the **scholar** rare species, the **photographer** very big fish and the **dreamer** tanks full of plants and decorations. A delighted visitor leaves more coins; a disappointed one walks off with a question mark. The **What the visitors think** box shows their opinion of each tank.

## The museum

Give the museum one fish of each species from the pen (**To the museum** button). It stays there for good and brings visitors and coins every hour. The museum opens **hall by hall**: pond hall, sea gallery, tropical greenhouse, polar hall and abyss hall; each hall opens after 8 gifts in the one before. The **hall of legends** opens with the third hall and waits for the legends of Fishing. A complete hall gives a big reward.

## The wallpaper

With the **Aquarium** theme (a separate plugin, in NeoStore > Themes), your finest tank becomes the NeoDex background, and its fish swim across the screen behind the panels. A tank's **Use as wallpaper** button picks it; otherwise the best-rated tank is used. The background is updated when you open Aquarium and stays on your PC (it does not go through sync).

## Log, challenges and stars

- **The log**: every species put in a tank enters the log of its water, with coins the first time. A quarter of a water: coins; half: a **unique decoration** (old stump, little lighthouse, tiki statue, igloo, abyssal vent); complete: a big reward.
- **Challenges**: three at a time, from putting 3 fish in a tank to opening a tank of every water. Each one done is claimed for coins.
- **Stars**: each tank earns up to **3 stars** (happy, well filled, varied, decorated or planted, a rare fish for the third). Each star adds 15 % to its income.

## Fish happiness

Each tank's Joy gauge rises when the fish are fed, the water is clean and there are decorations (up to four count) and plants, and drops when the tank is nearly full. The higher it is and the more fish there are, the more coins per hour the tank earns. The line at the top sums up your coins, the till and the hourly income of all your tanks.

## The shop

Everything is paid with Fishing coins:

- **Enlarge** a tank: small (6 places), medium (10 places, 900 coins), large (16 places, 3,500 coins).
- **Filter** (600 coins): the water gets dirty three times more slowly.
- **Feeder** (1,200 coins): feeds the tank by itself.
- **Decorations**: plant, rock, coral, amphora, castle, stone arch, chest, bubbler or wreck, six at most per tank (four count for joy). **Drag them** along the sand to place them.
- **New tank**: choose its type of water; each extra tank costs more than the previous one.

A fish you have no room for can be **released**: it gives back half its value. Click a fish in the tank to see its name.

## Data and sync

Your tanks, fish and coins are stored by NeoDex, backed up and synced between your PCs when sync is on. Aquarium needs the **Pêche** plugin, installed automatically with it: both share the same purse.

## Install

In NeoDex: **NeoStore → Discover → Aquarium → Install**.

Building a plugin: see the [NeoDex SDK](https://github.com/NDesumeur/neodex-sdk).
